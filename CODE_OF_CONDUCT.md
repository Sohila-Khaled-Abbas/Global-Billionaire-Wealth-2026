# Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers of the **Global Billionaire Wealth Analytics** project pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free and enriching experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Standards of Behavior

We encourage behaviors that foster an open and welcoming environment:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other contributors.
- Respecting differing opinions, perspectives, and analytical viewpoints.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community data integrity.
- Prioritizing rigorous, data-driven, and objective discussion of macroeconomic topics.

Unacceptable behaviors include:
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks.
- Harassment in any form, public or private.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement & Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate or harmful.

To report an issue or concern, please reach out to the project repository maintainers via GitHub Issues or private inquiry.
